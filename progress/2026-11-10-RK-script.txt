[serious] 1941. A 23-year-old woman lay AWAKE… while two doctors cut inside her brain. Her name was Rosemary Kennedy. What did they cut? 1 or 2… answer at the end.
[matter-of-fact] She was the sister of a future president, John F. Kennedy. She learned more slowly than her brothers and sisters. Her father arranged a NEW operation… and did not tell her mother.
[serious] Walter Freeman and James Watts went in through the top of her head. While they cut, she was asked to say a prayer… and to sing. Then her words STOPPED.
[serious] The answer: 1, the CONNECTIONS of the frontal lobe, the part that plans, decides and starts our words. After the operation, she could not walk, or speak clearly. Ever again.
[warmly] She lived 63 more years, mostly in a home in Wisconsin. Her sister Eunice later founded the Special Olympics. So… 1 or 2, did you get it? Dr. Mot, one brain story at a time.
