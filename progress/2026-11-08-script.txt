[curious] 1953. Surgeons removed part of a 27-year-old's brain… to stop his seizures. It worked. But it cost him something STRANGE. Which part did they take? 1 or 2… answer at the end.
[matter-of-fact] His name was Henry Molaison. Medicine called him H.M. After surgery he could not form ONE new memory. Meet him, leave the room, come back… and he meets you for the first time. Again.
[impressed] Then Brenda Milner gave him a test: trace a star… looking only in a MIRROR. Every day he got better. And every day he said he had NEVER done it before.
[serious] The answer: the HIPPOCAMPUS, on both sides. Remembering what happened… and learning how to do something… use different brain systems. When he died in 2008, his brain was cut into 2,401 slices… and the world finally learned his name.
[warmly] So… 1 or 2, did you get it? Dr. Mot, one brain story at a time… because the real question is…
