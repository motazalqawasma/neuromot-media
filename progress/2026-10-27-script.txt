[serious] Can a drug actually SLOW Alzheimer's? Honestly… this one surprised me.

[curious] In the TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2 trial, 1736 people with early Alzheimer's got monthly drips of donanemab… or PLACEBO, for 76 weeks. A computer picked who got what.

[matter-of-fact] In earlier disease, memory and daily life declined… 35% SLOWER. Basically, buying time.

[impressed] And the amyloid plaque? By 76 weeks it was CLEARED in… 80% of them. That's a better cleanup than my room during exam week.

[skeptical] But. Here's the catch. Brain swelling on MRI showed up in… 24% on the drug, versus about 2% on placebo. Most had no symptoms… but 3 deaths were LINKED to treatment.
