## Facts (checked 2026-11-13; re-check on video day with 1 more source, ideally Domanski 2013 or Dronkers 2007)
- Louis Victor Leborgne, born 21 Jul 1809, Moret (now Moret-sur-Loing), France. Father an elementary school teacher. (Smithsonian, LiveScience 2013)
- Craftsman (shoe/last maker per other sources; check) and epilepsy from childhood. (LiveScience)
- Lost speech at about 30; admitted to Bicêtre Hospital, Paris, 1840; lived there 21 years. (LiveScience, BrainFacts)
- Could only say one syllable: "tan" (nickname "Tan"). Right side of his body paralyzed. (BrainFacts)
- Broca met him in April 1861 at age 51; he died a few days later; Broca's autopsy: lesion in the LEFT FRONTAL lobe (third frontal convolution = Broca's area; confirm wording). (BrainFacts)
- Identified by Cezary W. Domanski, Maria Curie-Sklodowska University, Poland, Journal of the History of the Neurosciences, Jan 2013. (Smithsonian, LiveScience)
- 2007: Dronkers and French colleagues did high-resolution MRI of the preserved brains of Leborgne and Lelong: damage went deeper than Broca's area, including fibre pathways linking frontal and back language areas. Brain, 2007;130:1432 (check DOI 10.1093/brain/awm042). (BrainFacts)
- Brain preserved in Paris (Musée Dupuytren collection; check current place).
- MYTHS to avoid: do not say he was "unintelligent"; he understood speech. Do not invent his swear words unless a source quotes them.
