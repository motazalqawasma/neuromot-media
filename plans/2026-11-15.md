## Facts (checked 2026-11-14)
- Born 26 Jan 1891, Spokane, Washington; died 5 Apr 1976, Montreal. (Wikipedia; McGill The Neuro)
- 1928: learned local-anesthesia awake brain surgery with Otfried Foerster in Germany; moved to Montreal 1928. (Wikipedia; hearinghealthmatters)
- 1934: founded the Montreal Neurological Institute with Rockefeller Foundation money; first director until 1960. (McGill; Canadian Encyclopedia)
- Montreal procedure: patients awake under local anesthesia, cortex stimulated with an electrode to find where seizures start, for epilepsy drugs could not control. (McGill 200; qz)
- Brain tissue feels no pain; local anesthetic numbed skin and skull. (qz 2018)
- Some patients heard voices or music, and a small share (<5%) had vivid memory recall on temporal stimulation. (Penfield & Perot 1963; Wikipedia)
- Homunculus first published 1 Dec 1937, Penfield W, Boldrey E. Brain 1937;60(4):389-443, doi:10.1093/brain/60.4.389. Drawn by medical illustrator Hortense Cantlie. (Catani, Brain 2017, PMC5841206; Preul et al. World Neurosurg 2019)
- Big hands and face, small trunk/legs. (Catani 2017)
- Avoid: the "burnt toast" anecdote (not checked in a primary source).
