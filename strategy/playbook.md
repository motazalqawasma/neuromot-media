# #NeuroMot growth playbook (from Motaz's 5 growth prompts, 2026-10-31)

Niche: neurology & neurosurgery research explained in ~50 s. Audience: medical students, junior doctors, curious people (English, Arab world). Goal: reach + saves + shares + the first real comments.

## Prompt 1 — 7 saturated patterns in neuro / medical content
1. "5 signs of a stroke" style lists.
2. Brain "fun facts" and myths ("you only use 10%").
3. Med-student day-in-the-life / study-with-me vlogs.
4. Anatomy mnemonics and flashcards.
5. Shock surgery clips and "doctor reacts".
6. Talking-head "doctor explains X in 60 s".
7. Journal-club slides full of text (abstracts read aloud).

## 10 ideas that break these patterns (and still fit the algorithm)
| # | Core idea | Hook | Format | Why it feels new | Why people save / share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **You decide** — a real trial's two choices, reveal the result | "Operate now, or wait?" | A video + poll | viewer becomes the doctor | share to argue with a friend |
| 2 | **The trial that changed the guideline** — before/after | "Before 2023, we said no. Then this trial…" | carousel | a story, not a list | save for exams |
| 3 | **The number that lies** — relative vs absolute risk from the paper | "50% better… or 2 patients out of 100?" | E video | teaches you not to be fooled | save (stats lesson) |
| 4 | **Exam vs reality** — textbook answer vs new trial | "Your exam says X. The 2025 trial says…" | F video | med-student pain point | save + send to batch group |
| 5 | **Dr. Mot's case file** — case in 3 slides, answer in comments | "Man, 54, sudden weakness. What would you do?" | D carousel | a puzzle, not a lecture | comments (answers) |
| 6 | **Too late?** time-window series | "Too late to pull the clot?" | C video | series → people wait for the next | share |
| 7 | **1 paper, 3 levels** — for a kid / a student / a neurosurgeon | "Explained 3 ways in 30 s" | carousel | same fact, three depths | save + share |
| 8 | **The limitation nobody reads** — the catch first | "This trial looked perfect. Read line 12." | video | honesty builds trust | share to colleagues |
| 9 | **Stat in 30 s** — one stats idea (AUROC, NNT, P value) with this week's paper | "AUROC 0.90 — what does that even mean?" | text reel | useful forever | save |
| 10 | **Arabic edition** of the best video, in Motaz's cloned voice | Arabic two-option hook | video | almost no Arabic neuro-paper content | share in Arab med groups |

## Prompt 2 — Hook lab (now step 1B of the video task)
15 hooks in 5 groups (curiosity · contrarian · emotional tension · specificity · calm authority), facts only, no clickbait, rank the best 5, use #1 unless PLAN.md pins one.

## Prompt 3 — Faceless versions of each idea
Every paper already has 2 faceless versions: the video (Dr. Mot + captions) and the 6-slide carousel. Possible 3rd: a silent "text reel" (big words + numbers, sound effects only, ~15 s) — to test only if carousels keep beating videos.

## Prompt 4 — Retention pass (now in step 2 of the video task)
Hook in 1.5 s, open loop early, every line pulls to the next, cut weak lines, payoff near the end, last line loops, one save reason + one share reason.
