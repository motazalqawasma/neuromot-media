# NeuroMot lessons — weak points, mistakes, fixes

After every published video, the run compares ALL past videos and carousels, names the 3 biggest weak points (views, hook, retention, likes, comments, followers), the mistake behind each, 1–3 fixes for the next video, and judges whether earlier fixes worked. Newest at the top.

## 2026-11-03 (after ENRICH, Paper #9 — first weak-point review)

Numbers used: TikTok from Metricool today; Instagram/Facebook/YouTube from the 2026-11-02 platform check (log.md). Browser was offline at 15:45, so no fresh platform pages.

**Earlier fixes — verdict**
- Two-option hook: WORKED (early sign) — the 2 best Instagram 3-s rates are both two-option hooks (ASDH 31.6%, OSCIS 28.9%; others 21.7–27.2%).
- Stroke/neurosurgery first: WORKED (early sign) — Facebook OSCIS 171 vs Eptinezumab 37 / Donanemab 33.

- 1/2 comment question in caption + end screen + our own comment: DID NOT WORK — 0 real viewer comments after 9 videos and 5 carousels.

**3 biggest weak points**
1. COMMENTS: 0 real comments on every app (TikTok comments = 0 on all 14 posts). Mistake: the question comes only in the last ~8 s (most viewers left at 5–8 s), and our own question comment is posted by hand hours later or not at all.
2. RETENTION: Instagram average watch 5.0–8.2 s on 48–62 s videos (= viewers see ~10–15%); 68–78% leave in the first 3 s. Mistake: the strongest number appears at ~20 s, after setup; frames 0–0.4 s are a static cover.
3. LIKES / FOLLOWS: TikTok likes per 100 views 0.4–1.9% (SELECT2 8/432, OSCIS 2/228, Eptinezumab 2/173); no reason given to follow. Mistake: no "payoff + save/follow" moment in the middle, and no series that makes people want the next video.

**Fixes for the next video (Thu 2026-11-05) — applied without asking (Motaz's rule)**
1. Ask the 1/2 question EARLY: on screen at ~8–10 s ("Comment 1 or 2 — answer at the end"), not only at the end; and set Metricool firstCommentText = the question on Instagram + Facebook so it is the automatic first comment the second the post goes live.
2. Shocking number in the first 2 s, next to the hook (e.g. "18% → 9.3%"); target 35–40 s (85–100 words) and cut every setup sentence.
3. Series + follow reason: brand the decision videos as "OPERATE OR WAIT? · case N" and end with "Follow for case N+1" instead of a generic follow line.
